The Night Booka sky

The Night Book is a sky. It is one real canvas, painted slowly, where every work I make leaves a single star in gold leaf. The work belongs to the person it was made for, whether it is a canvas, a portrait, an evening or a wall. The star stays. It carries no name and no words, only a mark and a number. When two works share something, a door, a flood, a staircase, I draw a line between their stars. Over the years, strangers who never met form constellations. Jung called the layer of dreaming we all share the collective unconscious. This is my map of it.
