About the Book

The Night Book takes letters for sittings — dreams and recurring visions, presence portraits, live rooms, and murals. Apply only. A handful of offers each year. Work ships by crate. I travel in March (Europe) and September (Americas).

This is an interpretive studio, not a likeness mill and not a reading service. The picture is what the sitting gives the work.
